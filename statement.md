Statement
Problem Statement:
Many people want a quick way to check their Body Mass Index (BMI) and get simple, general guidance on healthy habits, but most existing tools are require internet access, or bundle in unnecessary features. There's a need for a lightweight, offline, beginner-friendly  application that calculates BMI accurately and pairs it with basic educational information on nutrition, daily routines, and what BMI actually means.
BMI & Healthy Routine Assistant solves this by providing a simple, menu-driven Python program that calculates BMI from user-provided weight and height, validates inputs, and offers general educational tips  all in one place.

->Scope of the Project
1.Calculating BMI from weight (kg) and height (cm)
2.Categorizing BMI results with simple feedback
3.Validating user input (rejecting invalid, negative, or zero values)
4.Providing general food suggestion tips
5.Providing general daily routine guidance (morning, daytime, evening, night)
6.Explaining the BMI formula and its limitations as an educational feature
7.A menu-driven console interface for navigating between features

High-Level Features
1.BMI Calculator: accepts weight and height, computes BMI, and displays the result with its category
2.Food Suggestions: general balanced meal ideas for educational purposes
3.Healthy Daily Routine: simple time of day routine guidance
4.BMI Information: explains the BMI formula and its role as a general screening measure, not a complete health assessment
5.Menu-Driven Navigation: a single entry point (main.py) that lets users move between all features easily
